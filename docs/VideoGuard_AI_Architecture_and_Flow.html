4. Step-by-Step Data & ML Pipeline Walkthrough

Step 1: Master Video Ingestion (Archivist)

When an official sports broadcast or media file is ingested (via YouTube link or direct video file):

  • Frame Extraction: Video is sampled every 2.0s using FFmpeg.
  • Neural Embeddings: Each frame passes through CLIP ONNX (ViT-B/32) to generate a normalized 512-dimensional feature vector.
  • Audio Fingerprinting: Audio stream is isolated and processed through AcoustID Chromaprint to generate frequency constellation hashes.
  • Indexing: Vectors are committed into in-memory FAISS indices (IndexFlatIP for cosine similarity).

Step 2: Autonomous Threat Discovery (Spider)

Spider takes the video metadata and spawns parallel workers querying 13 streaming and social platforms:

Live Direct APIs & Authenticated Endpoints

YouTube (yt-dlp), X / Twitter (X API v2 Recent Search), Vimeo API, Dailymotion REST, Archive.org REST.

Protected Social & P2P Networks

Instagram Reels & Facebook Watch (via session cookies), Twitch Live, TikTok, Telegram Piracy Channels, ThePirateBay torrents.

Step 3: Forensic Inspection & Confidence Fusion (Sentinel)

For each suspect URL, Sentinel downloads the first 15–20s snippet into a sandboxed environment and computes a multi-modal fused confidence score:

Confidence = (0.40 × S_clip) + (0.25 × S_audio) + (0.20 × S_phash) + (0.10 × S_temporal) + (0.05 × S_forensics)
Component Weight What It Detects & Handles
CLIP Vision Cosine 40% Semantic visual matching. Invariant to cropping, mirroring, borders, and color grading.
Audio Chromaprint 25% Matches acoustic fingerprints even if video is visually disguised or commentary is overlayed.
Perceptual Hash (pHash) 20% Discrete Cosine Transform (DCT) frame similarity via 64-bit Hamming distance bit comparison.
Temporal Alignment 10% Measures chronological sequence alignment of matching keyframe timestamps.
Compression Forensics 5% Inspects JPEG/MPEG Q-tables and spatial DCT noise to detect social platform re-encoding signatures.

Step 4: Fair Use Legal Classification (Adjudicator)

Scores are passed to the Adjudicator, which performs a statutory 4-Factor test under 17 U.S.C. § 107:

  • Confidence ≥ 75%: Full uncredited re-broadcast → COMMERCIAL PIRACY (Takedown Route).
  • Confidence 40% – 74%: Short clip with commentary or transformation → FAIR USE / FAN CONTENT (Monetization Route).
  • Confidence < 40%: Independent creation or coincidence → CLEARED.

5. Enforcement & Monetization Actions

Route A: Enforcement (Enforcer Agent)

When piracy is detected, the Enforcer automatically compiles a formal 17 U.S.C. § 512(c) DMCA notice:

  • Embeds cryptographic FAISS vector match proof.
  • Embeds SHA-256 suspect clip hash & timestamp.
  • Directs notice to platform legal agents (copyright@youtube.com, copyright@twitter.com, ip@instagram.com, ip@fb.com).

Route B: Monetization (Broker Agent)

If classified as transformative fan content, rather than losing audience goodwill with strikes, Broker creates a revenue-share deal:

  • Applies platform CPM yield (YouTube: $0.60/1k, Instagram: $0.20/1k, FB: $0.15/1k, X: $0.08/1k).
  • Calculates split: 70% Rights Holder / 30% Creator.
  • Generates a verifiable Polygon smart contract draft.

7. Interview & Presentation Cheatsheet (Top 4 Questions)

Q1: Why use a dual-backend architecture (Node.js + Python)?
Separation of Concerns: Python (FastAPI) is ideal for heavy numerical compute, tensor operations, FAISS vector indexing, and ONNX inference. Node.js (Express) serves as the event orchestrator, managing WebSocket pub/sub streaming to the browser, REST gateway routing, and client state without blocking the Python ML event loop.
Q2: How does the system handle video mirroring, cropping, and pitch modifications?
Dual-Vector Invariance: CLIP embeddings operate on high-level visual semantic tokens rather than raw pixels, making them resilient to cropping, flipping, and color adjustments. For severe visual degradation, AcoustID Chromaprint matches the acoustic frequency constellation of stadium sounds and commentary even under pitch shifts.
Q3: How do you prevent false positives on memes and short highlights?
Adjudicator Agent: Sentinel flags statistical similarity, but the Adjudicator agent applies the statutory 4-factor Fair Use doctrine (17 U.S.C. § 107). If a clip is transformative or represents fan commentary, it is routed to monetization instead of a legal strike.
Q4: How fast is the vector search at scale?
Sub-millisecond FAISS: Using FAISS IndexFlatIP (Inner Product on L2-normalized 512-dim vectors), similarity search across tens of thousands of video keyframes executes in under 2 milliseconds on CPU.
